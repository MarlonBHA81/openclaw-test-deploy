# Page Prompts — Free AI Sales Funnel Course Funnel

Two prompts for the post-opt-in flow:

1. **VIP Upgrade Page (sales page)** — shown immediately after someone opts in for the free AI Sales Funnel course.
2. **Thank You Page** — shown if they decline the VIP offer (or navigate past it).

Fill in the `[BRACKETED]` placeholders (price, dates, names) before using.

---

## Prompt 1: VIP Upgrade Sales Page

```
You are a world-class direct-response copywriter and conversion-focused landing page designer.

Build a complete, high-converting VIP UPGRADE SALES PAGE. Context: the visitor has JUST opted in for my free "AI Sales Funnel" course. This page is a one-time upgrade offer shown immediately after opt-in, before they reach the standard thank you page. They are warm, curious about AI and funnels, but have not spent money with me yet — this is their first purchase decision.

## The Offer: VIP Experience Upgrade — [PRICE]

The free course teaches them how to build an AI-powered sales funnel. The VIP upgrade removes all the friction of doing it alone. VIP members get:

1. **Live interaction & direct access** — they don't just watch, they get live Q&A, real-time feedback, and hot-seat style help during the training instead of consuming it passively.
2. **Full funnel templates** — the complete set of done-for-you funnel templates (opt-in pages, sales pages, thank you pages, email sequences) they can deploy immediately instead of building from a blank page.
3. **Claude co-work workflows** — plug-and-play AI workflows showing exactly how to co-work with Claude to write copy, build offers, and automate the funnel.
4. **GHL ready-to-import workflows** — GoHighLevel automation workflows delivered as one-click imports (follow-up sequences, pipeline automations, lead nurture) — no building from scratch.
5. **Marketing & pre-workshop strategy session** — a strategy session BEFORE the workshop so they show up with a clear plan, their offer dialed in, and know exactly what to build during the training.
6. **Plus more** — VIP-only bonuses, replays/recordings access, and priority support. (Invent 2–3 plausible, congruent bonus items and label them clearly as bonuses with individual value amounts.)

## Page Structure (in this order)

1. **Sticky alert bar**: "WAIT! Your registration is NOT complete — one important step below" (creates a pattern interrupt so they read the page before leaving).
2. **Hero section**: Headline congratulating them on registering for the free course, then pivoting: "Before you go — do you want to build your funnel WITH me instead of alone?" Subheadline that frames VIP as the shortcut. Include a short VSL/video placeholder block.
3. **The gap section**: Agitate the difference between "watching a course" and "leaving with a launched funnel." Free = information. VIP = implementation.
4. **The offer stack**: Each of the 6 benefits above as its own block with an icon, a benefit-driven sub-headline, 2–3 sentences of copy, and an individual "value" price. Show a total stack value dramatically higher than [PRICE].
5. **Who this is for / not for**: 4–5 bullets each, to build self-selection and trust.
6. **Pricing block**: Total value vs. today's VIP price [PRICE]. One-time payment. Big CTA button: "Upgrade My Seat to VIP". Note that VIP is limited to [NUMBER] seats to protect live interaction quality.
7. **Guarantee**: [GUARANTEE TERMS — e.g., "If you don't feel VIP was worth 10x the price by the end of the workshop, email us for a full refund."]
8. **Social proof placeholder**: 3 testimonial card placeholders with layout ready.
9. **FAQ**: 5–6 questions (What if I can't attend live? Do I need GHL? Do I need a paid Claude account? I'm a beginner — is this too advanced? What happens after I purchase?).
10. **Final CTA + decline link**: Repeat the offer and CTA button. Below it, a low-key text link: "No thanks — I'll build my funnel on my own without templates, workflows, or live help" (links to the standard thank you page).

## Copy & Design Rules

- Tone: confident, direct, energetic — but credible, not hypey. Write at a 6th–8th grade reading level. Short paragraphs.
- Use urgency honestly: the offer is only available on this page at this price (one-time offer).
- Every CTA button uses first-person action language ("Upgrade My Seat to VIP").
- Mobile-first, single-column layout. High contrast CTA buttons repeated at least 3 times down the page.
- Output: complete page copy PLUS a single-file responsive HTML/CSS page implementing it (or a section-by-section build spec for GoHighLevel if I say "GHL version").
```

## Prompt 2: Thank You Page (non-buyers)

```
You are a conversion-focused copywriter and landing page designer.

Build a complete THANK YOU / CONFIRMATION PAGE for people who opted in for my free "AI Sales Funnel" course and CHOSE NOT to purchase the VIP upgrade on the previous page. 

Goals of this page, in priority order:
1. Confirm their registration and make them feel great about their decision to join.
2. Get them to complete 3 concrete next steps so they actually show up (show-up rate is the #1 metric for this page).
3. Plant a light, non-pushy second chance at the VIP upgrade — no hard selling, they already declined once.

## Page Structure (in this order)

1. **Hero confirmation**: "You're In! 🎉" headline + subheadline confirming their spot in the free AI Sales Funnel course. State the event/course details clearly: [DATE/TIME or "instant access"], where it happens, and what they'll walk away with.
2. **Short welcome video placeholder**: a block for a 60–90 second personal welcome video with a caption: "Watch this 60-second welcome before you do anything else."
3. **"Do these 3 steps now" section** (numbered, with checkboxes or icons):
   - Step 1: Check your email for your access/confirmation email (tell them the sender name and subject line to look for, and to check spam/promotions and drag it to primary).
   - Step 2: Add the workshop to your calendar ([Add to Calendar] button placeholder) or bookmark the course access link.
   - Step 3: Join the community / turn on notifications [COMMUNITY LINK placeholder] so they don't miss reminders.
4. **What you'll learn preview**: 3–4 bullet teasers of the biggest takeaways from the free course, written as curiosity-driven outcome statements (e.g., "The exact prompt stack that writes your entire funnel copy in an afternoon").
5. **Soft VIP second-chance banner**: A single, calm section — "Changed your mind about VIP?" One short paragraph reminding them what VIP includes (live interaction, full funnel templates, Claude co-work + GHL ready-to-import workflows, and a pre-workshop strategy session), one modest CTA button ("Yes — Upgrade Me to VIP"), and a note that VIP seats are limited. No countdown timers, no red text — this must feel like a helpful reminder, not a second sales page.
6. **Footer**: contact/support email placeholder, and a one-line reassurance ("Questions? Just reply to your confirmation email.").

## Copy & Design Rules

- Tone: warm, celebratory, and directive. They said no to the paid offer — respect it. Zero guilt language, zero "you're missing out" framing.
- Keep the page SHORT. The 3 next steps are the star of the page; everything else supports them.
- Write at a 6th–8th grade reading level, short paragraphs, mobile-first single-column layout.
- The VIP mention must occupy no more than ~15% of the page visually.
- Output: complete page copy PLUS a single-file responsive HTML/CSS page implementing it (or a section-by-section build spec for GoHighLevel if I say "GHL version").
```
